Approach

How a campaign runs with me.

The same four steps on every account, whether the budget is 500 EGP or a few hundred thousand.

01

Plan the funnel

Understand the offer and the customer journey, then set the objective, KPIs, content calendar and creative briefs.

02

Structure to compare

Segment audiences and split campaigns by area, placement or service so cost differences show up in the data instead of hiding in an average.

03

Test creative

Work with designers on platform-specific ads, A/B test creatives and copy, and keep the winners running.

04

Report & optimize

Track ROAS, CPL, CTR and CPC, report clearly through dashboards, and move budget toward what works.
